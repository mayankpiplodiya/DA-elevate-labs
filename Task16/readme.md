Insights: 

1) Sales show a clear time-based pattern, indicating that demand changes over different months rather than remaining constant.

2) Certain months record higher sales consistently, suggesting the presence of seasonal demand peaks.

3) Low sales periods are also repeated at regular intervals, indicating off-season months.

4) The overall trend indicates whether the business is growing or declining over time, which is important for long-term planning.

5) The rolling mean smooths short-term fluctuations and confirms the underlying trend direction.

6) Sudden spikes or drops in sales may indicate promotions, holidays, stock issues, or external market factors.

7) The time-based train-test split ensures the model is evaluated in a realistic forecasting scenario rather than random sampling.

8) The Exponential Smoothing model captures recent patterns effectively, making it suitable for short-term forecasting.

9) The MAE value shows the average forecasting error in actual sales units, helping measure practical accuracy.

10) The MAPE value indicates the percentage error, which helps understand how reliable the forecast is for business decision making
