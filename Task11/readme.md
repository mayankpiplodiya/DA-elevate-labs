3-The conversion rate for users who viewed advertisements is 2.55 percent.

4-The conversion rate for the control group who viewed public service announcements is 1.79 percent.

6-The p-value of 1.99e-13 is well below the significance threshold of 0.05, confirming the results are statistically significant.

7-The 95 percent confidence interval for the difference in conversion rates ranges from 0.0059 to 0.0094, excluding zero and confirming a positive impact.

8-The null hypothesis is rejected, providing strong evidence that the marketing campaign effectively influences user behavior.

9-Friday was identified as the most frequent day when users were exposed to the highest volume of advertisements.

10-The business recommendation is to scale the advertisement campaign as it demonstrates a clear and statistically valid increase in conversion performance.
